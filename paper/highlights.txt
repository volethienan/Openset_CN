Highlights
- NIDS adapts only by retracting a novelty prior where analysts confirm benign traffic
- Unlabelled traffic cannot move the detector; analyst errors have a bounded footprint
- Best average zero-day detection against five recent detectors on four benchmarks
- 25 labels per day outperform a continual learner using 200 to 400 labels per day
- Counterfactual isolates trusted evidence as the cause of robustness to poisoning
