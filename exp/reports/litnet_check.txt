LITNET-2020: examined as a candidate real-network dataset and not used (10 October 2026)

Dataset: Lithuanian research and education network, NetFlow v9, 39,603,674 flows on 50 calendar days between
2019-03-06 and 2020-01-31, 12 annotated attack types (allFlows.csv of ALLinONE.zip). Stream built with the protocol
of the other streams (builder and runs: commit a528851): anchor = capture days 2019-03-06 and 2019-10-02
(udp_f, udp_reaper_w known), 10 zero-day attack types afterwards, 403,108 sampled flows, 22 per-flow features (the 19
rule-output columns of the dataset excluded, because they encode the annotation) and 10 context features.

Check runs, seed 0 (reports/litnet_check_runs.csv):
    method   F1     MCC    FPR    zero-day recall  AUROC
    ERNP     0.296  0.244  0.377  0.784            0.685
    kNN      0.397  0.372  0.256  0.839            0.811
    IForest  0.042  0.005  0.024  0.026            0.816
Zero-day recall at 1% stream FPR is below 0.01 for the three detectors.

Reason for not using it: the 'normal' class is not a benign class. The annotation marks 12 attack types by rules; all
other traffic, including Internet-wide scanning and the part of an attack that the rules miss, is published as normal.
The flows of the 'normal' class that ERNP alerts on are concentrated as follows (seed 0):
    2019-12-03   74% come from four hosts of 104.152.52.0/24 (single UDP / SYN probes)
    2020-01-25   99.8% come from three hosts (80.82.77.132, 80.82.77.221, 45.143.220.169): SYN-only flows, the profile
                 of the attack annotated on that day (tcp_w32_w)
    2020-01-30   99.2% go to one host (193.219.81.138): SYN+ACK from ports below 1024, the profile of the attacks
                 annotated on that day (tcp_land, tcp_red_w)
    2020-01-31   mixed, mostly UDP
A false-positive rate measured against this class does not measure false alarms, for any novelty detector, and the
problem is spread over many days and hosts, so that no single documented exclusion repairs it (unlike the one
unlabelled scan of RoEduNet-SIMARGL2021). The decision rests on the validity of the labels; the check runs above are
reported so that the exclusion can be judged.
